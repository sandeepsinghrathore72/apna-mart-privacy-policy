Apna Mart Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Apna Mart respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our grocery delivery application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Mobile number used for login and OTP verification.
  • Order details, including products and quantities ordered.
  • Information you provide when placing an order.
  • Technical information required for app operation and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your mobile number and provide login access.
  • To process and manage grocery orders.
  • To contact you regarding your orders and support requests.
  • To improve application performance and security.

3. Data Storage and Sharing

Order and account information may be stored on our backend servers and database to provide the services. We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the application or fulfil your orders.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide our services and meet applicable legal requirements. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us.

6. Children's Privacy

Apna Mart is not specifically designed for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
